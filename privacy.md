# Privacy Policy

## The app

Work Clock does not collect any data or personal information. It makes no network requests. Your settings (timezone, time format and so on) are stored locally on your Mac and never leave it.

## The website

The website, work-clock.jabran.dev, uses Google Analytics to count visits and downloads. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects anonymous usage data such as pages visited, browser type and approximate location. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

Downloads are served by GitHub, which may log requests under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
